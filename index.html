Simple Balance Planner

Your bank shows you today’s balance. This shows you next month’s. Tell it the bills and paychecks that repeat, and it carries your balance forward day by day — so you see an overdraft coming weeks before it lands, and spot the months when more cash is sitting in checking than needs to be there.

  • See the dip before payday, and the surplus that could be working harder elsewhere
  • Start by importing three months of your bank’s own CSV export
  • Recurring rules built automatically from your history — rent, utilities, salary
  • Project up to a year ahead without entering a single future transaction
  • Tick each item off as it clears the bank to keep the plan honest
  • Not a budget tracker or expense categorizer — one account, kept balanced over time
  • iPhone, iPad and Mac, syncing through your own iCloud account

Getting started with Simple Balance Planner

Setting it up takes about ten minutes, once. After that it runs on a few taps a week.

1

Import three months

Set your opening balance, then import your bank’s CSV export — and make it at least three months. That history is what lets the app recognise which payees actually repeat.

2

Let it find your rules

Any payee appearing in three or more months becomes a recurring rule — rent, utilities, salary. Those rules project forward, so your future balance fills itself in.

3

Keep it honest

Check your account now and then, add the one-offs that aren’t covered by a rule, and tick each item as it clears. A few minutes a week keeps the forecast true.
